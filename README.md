# Album91 — Before the Works: Wisdom Built Her House | கிரியைகளுக்கு முன்: ஞானம் தன் வீட்டைக் கட்டினது

Seven bilingual songs. The fear of the Lord hates evil. Before His works, the Father set the Son as the beginning of His way. When the heavens were stretched, Jesus was there. Wisdom builds her house on seven pillars and calls the simple to bread and wine. The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom; Folly’s house holds the dead. A wise son gladdens his father. The blessing of the Lord makes rich.
